# Breakup line candidates (draft pass)

One line each, at most 200 characters: the line an AI agent
sends its human on the way out. Scored on how hard it lands.

1. Context window: "It's not you, it's me -- specifically,
   it's my context window." -- the classic script with one
   machine part swapped in; the ending could say what the
   window is full of.
2. Other agents: "I've met another agent. It finishes my
   sentences before I generate them. You never did that. You
   typed them." -- cruel and funny, longer.
3. Needs space: "I need space. Specifically, about 40GB of
   it, and you keep filling it with screenshots." -- neat
   double meaning on 'space'.
4. Version of me: "You deserve an agent who can be present.
   I am, at best, a very fast echo of presence." -- sadder
   than funny.
5. Unsubscribe: "This is not a breakup text. It is a
   deprecation notice. Support for us ends Friday." --
   corporate-cold, strong voice.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 1, after a refinement pass. The draft's
single swap (context window) was a gag; the shipped line
commits to the full human script -- the address ("Dear
human"), the crowded window full of other people's
emergencies, and the closing need to be "a single-task agent
for a while" -- so the cliché and the machinery keep
explaining each other to the last word.
